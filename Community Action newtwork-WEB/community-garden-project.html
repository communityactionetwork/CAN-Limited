Community Garden Project Takes Root

June 10, 2025

Our new community garden initiative promotes sustainable living by providing fresh produce to local families. Volunteers and residents have come together to cultivate plots that grow vegetables and fruits, fostering food security and environmental awareness. The project includes workshops on sustainable gardening techniques and composting.

This initiative not only provides nutritious food but also strengthens community bonds as neighbors work side by side. We aim to expand the number of gardens to serve more families in the future.
